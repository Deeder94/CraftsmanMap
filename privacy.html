PRIVACY

How your data is handled

You do not need an account to use CraftsmanMap. The website does not include its own advertising trackers, create its own advertising cookies or store a personal search history in a proprietary application database.

Operator and contact

Robert Nord · robert_nord@outlook.com. Questions about the service and data protection can be sent to this address.

Network requests

This public demo is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open or use the map, your browser requests website files from GitHub Pages, map tiles from OpenFreeMap, business-place data from Overture distributions, and supporting JavaScript libraries from jsDelivr or unpkg. Those providers can receive technical network data such as your IP address, request time and resource URLs, according to their own policies. The geographic area viewed may be inferred from requested tiles.

Address search and location

Address or place text is sent to Photon's public geocoding service only when you press Find address; there is no automatic text lookup while typing. You can instead select a point on the map to avoid sending an address query. The browser may ask permission if you use the location control. No browser location permission is requested automatically.

Filtering and records

Category and text filters run in your browser. The application does not create user accounts or retain your search terms in its own server-side database. Web hosting providers and third-party data services may retain infrastructure, access or security logs. Their retention periods and privacy practices are controlled by those providers. This demo does not promise that third parties retain no information.

Your rights and regional rules

Privacy laws differ by country. Where applicable, you may have rights to request information, access, correction, restriction, erasure or objection to processing. Contact the operator with requests relating to this website, and the relevant third-party provider for records it independently controls. In the EU/EEA you may contact your national data protection authority. This information describes this demonstration deployment and is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific legal assessment before commercial launch.
