Privacy · GDPR

Privacy and personal information

CraftsmanMap is a free map-based discovery service. No account is required, and we do not build a proprietary customer or visitor database. This page describes our own processing; for external organisations' current policies, we link directly to their documents.

Operator and contact

For the processing purposes and means that we determine for this service, the operator's contact is Robert Nord: robert_nord@outlook.com. Hosting and data suppliers are separate organisations; see Sources & providers.

Information used and purpose

We use Overture Maps Places to show business names, available website links, coordinates, categories and straight-line distance. Filtering and categorisation help visitors discover services. Some names may identify an individual, such as a sole trader. Source map tiles can temporarily contain more attributes than are displayed.

Where GDPR applies to processing that we control, we assess legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) as the appropriate basis for a searchable service, subject to safeguards and the ability to object. We do not make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

Search and browser processing

Text/category filters and distance sorting operate within your browser. We do not operate user accounts, stored search histories, stored location histories or customer profiles. Temporary data may exist in browser memory or cache. Only when you submit an address search is the text sent to Photon. You can choose a point on the map instead. Location permission is requested only if you activate the location feature.

Hosting, recipients and international data flows

Our current website hosting provider is GitHub Pages. Browsing also triggers requests to external map, place-data and library services. These requests may involve technical data such as IP addresses and requested resources, potentially outside the EEA. We do not guarantee storage in a particular country. Refer to the suppliers' current official privacy and service documents for their own processing, retention, recipients and international transfers; we do not control these documents.

Retention and contact requests

Our map application does not maintain a persistent database of visitors' searches or business listings. If you email us, we process the details you send to respond to the request and handle applicable legal rights, keeping them only while necessary for the request or relevant legal duties. External suppliers' logs and retention are described by those suppliers.

Information for people named in listings (GDPR Article 14)

Where a listing identifies a person, the information comes indirectly from Overture Maps Places. Fields shown are name, website where available, coordinates, categorisation and distance. Listings are visible to map visitors. Our own processing uses the assessment described above; this public notice does not remove any additional Article 14 obligations that may apply in a particular case.

Your rights, corrections and objections

Contact robert_nord@outlook.com to request access, correction, erasure, restriction or to object, where applicable. Please include a business reference or location to help identify the entry. We can review a request to suppress a listing in our results independently of Overture's underlying data. Requests are handled within applicable deadlines, normally one month. For source-data errors see Overture support. You may complain to the competent data protection authority, such as Sweden's IMY.

Current release and provider policies

There are no advertising trackers or user-profiling systems implemented by this version of our application. We link directly to the sources and providers instead of duplicating their changing policy texts. Changes in hosting, data sources, analytics or advertising require review of this notice before deployment.
