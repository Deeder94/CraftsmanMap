ABOUT · METHODS AND SOURCES

Find the right help, wherever you are

CraftsmanMap helps people find contractors, home services, consultants, relevant retailers and equipment providers near a location anywhere the underlying data is available. It is a search and discovery tool, not a verified business directory or recommendation service.

How to search

Select Search the map and place a point on the map. The search begins automatically. Choose a radius of 500 m, 1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 km. Larger searches may take longer. You can also submit an address or place name and choose one of the suggested locations. Results are ordered by straight-line distance, not driving distance or travel time.

Global coverage and classification

The map has no country restriction. Search coverage and the availability of names, categories, addresses, websites and contact details vary significantly by country and region. The service groups likely relevant listings into 13 categories. Categories are based on Overture taxonomy and selected names, brands and profile information; they are not local business licensing classifications. Filtering operates on downloaded results, not a government register.

Data providers

Business listings come from Overture Maps Places, release when available. The base map is supplied by OpenFreeMap using map information from OpenStreetMap. Address suggestions come from the public Photon demo geocoder based on OpenStreetMap. No national company registry is queried by this demo.

Limits and reliability

Data may be incomplete, outdated, inaccurately classified or missing entirely. Places marked permanently closed and places with explicitly low confidence may be excluded. Some results are classified partly by business name or brand; a correct classification or real-world location is not guaranteed. A search that cannot retrieve all required Overture tiles is shown as partial, never as a verified complete result.

Public Overture PMTiles and Photon are demonstration-grade services without performance or availability guarantees. Near the poles (outside the standard Web Mercator map projection), searching is not supported. Very large searches at high latitudes may exceed the current tile limit.

Corrections and contact

Overture aggregates source records. Report errors in the source data to Overture. CraftsmanMap is responsible for its own selection and presentation logic, but does not edit the upstream Overture database. Before contracting, independently check identity, business status, required local permits or licences, insurance, service scope and terms.
